This file exists to document the schema, not to say anything. It is `status: draft`, so it
is never published, never routed, and never counted toward the threshold that reveals the
filter interface. Copy it, change every field, delete this paragraph.

## Frontmatter

`topic` and `level` both come from closed vocabularies in `src/content/writing/schema.ts`.
A value outside those lists fails the build rather than rendering a chip that links to a
route which does not exist. `tags` stays free-form for anything finer-grained.

`publishedAt` is required once `status` becomes `published`, and must not be in the future.

## Headings become the table of contents

Every `##` and `###` in the body is collected into the contents panel that appears beside
the article on wide screens. Two headings are the minimum before the panel renders at all.

### Third-level headings nest

They appear indented under the second-level heading above them.

## What not to do here

Do not leave placeholder prose that reads like real content. An unfinished draft that looks
finished is worse than an obviously empty one, because it can be published by accident and
nobody notices the claims are hollow.
