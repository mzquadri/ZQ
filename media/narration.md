# Narration script — Legal Knowledge Platform

For `media/legal-knowledge-platform.mp4` (1920×1080, 64.4 s).

The video ships **without audio**. This script exists so the same cut can be voiced later, and so
the captions have a written source. Timings are the shot holds in
`tools/record-legal-kb-video.mjs`; a comfortable read is about 2.5 words per second, and every
line below fits its shot at that pace.

Nothing here names an employer system, a corpus, or a quantity. The same privacy boundary that
governs the page governs this script.

## Audio

None. No music, no voiceover, no sound effects — nothing licensed, downloaded, or generated. The
cut is paced to be read, and silence suited a piece about restraint better than a bed of stock
music would have. If it is ever voiced, the table above is the script.
